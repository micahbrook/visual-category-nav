    How these were made

    • Every product is a Harvey Norman AU catalogue photo, cut out and placed to the rules measured from the Figma row.
    • The tiles around them are captured from Templates 1.0 at 100%, so the comparison is like for like.
    • Nothing was changed in Figma. The file was only viewed.

    To use one

    • Pick an option for each tile, then rebuild it in the Figma frame from the products listed on its card.
    • A and B for Beds reuse the bed already in the tile, so only one product changes.
    • Check the folded sheets at phone size (88px tiles), where thin layers can merge.
