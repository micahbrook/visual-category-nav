Harvey Norman category navs

Every row of category tiles or icons found on harveynorman.com.au: six on the live site today, and twelve earlier ones from the Internet Archive, newest first. None of the homepage snapshots from 2016 to 2026 has one; they’re on the sale and event pages and a few category pages.

The longest-running one is “Deals by category” on Hot Deals: icon tiles since at least November 2019, with seasonal icons (Christmas in 2019, palm trees in summer 2020, flames from 2022). Clearance has used the same tiles since 2021, and the Sale page has had a line-icon row since 2025.
